TRAVEL2: China Self-Drive – Seven Sacred Mountains

30 Nov – 13 Dec 2026 · Zeekr 7X EV · ~2,900 km

🏠 Home

⚠️ Before you go

  • Kunlun (昆仑山) is left out. It is in Qinghai/Xinjiang, about 3,500 km from Shanghai. It needs a separate flight trip (e.g. to Golmud).
  • Chinese temporary driving licence required. A Singapore licence is not valid. It needs a medical check, passport, Singapore licence and translation (about half a day in Shanghai). Confirm the age limit for a 70-year-old first.
  • Rent the Zeekr locally (check the 7X is available) and ask about one-way drop fees and winter tyres.

💡 Tips

  • Charging: winter cuts range 25–30%. Plan on ~350 km between charges, use fast chargers at service areas, arrive at the hotel with 20%+.
  • Winter: snow and ice at Huangshan, Wudang, Lushan and Taishan. Check cable-car closures.
  • Long days (Days 8 and 10): start by 7:30 and stop every 2 hours. Break Day 8 at Wuhan if tired.
  • Apps: Amap (高德), WeChat, Alipay (link your Singapore card before leaving).
  • Book ahead: hotels and cable-car tickets for Huangshan and Taishan.
